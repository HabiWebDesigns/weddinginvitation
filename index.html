Good to Know

Where should I park?

Free guest parking is available at the garden entrance. Shuttle service runs from the main gate every 15 minutes.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves to the covered pavilion. Bring a small umbrella just in case.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones. Please let us know in your RSVP so we can set a seat for them.

Can I take photos?

Yes, after the ceremony. During the vows we invite you to be fully present and unplugged.

Is there a gift registry?

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you wish to give, a wishing well will be at the reception.
